# Onyx 0.6.12

**Before you download:** Onyx needs either the Claude Code CLI signed in to a paid claude.ai plan, or the Codex CLI signed in with ChatGPT. Onyx does not use API keys or metered API billing. This release runs on Apple Silicon (arm64) Macs with macOS 13 or newer.

On 0.6.5 or later, choose **Onyx ▸ Check for Updates…** and it installs this one for you. On anything earlier, download `Onyx-0.6.12-macOS-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag Onyx to Applications. The app is signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple. To verify a download, place its matching `.sha256` file beside it and run `shasum -a 256 -c <filename>.sha256`.

## Editing (⌘E) types as Obsidian does

- **Lists continue.** Enter carries on a bullet, a numbered list (renumbered), a `- [ ]` task or a `>` quote; Enter on an empty item ends the list or takes it out a level. Shift+Enter is a new line inside the item, and Tab indents with a tab.
- **Pairs close themselves.** Brackets, quotes, `**` and `` ` `` close as you type, a closer steps over its twin, and Backspace takes an empty pair. A symbol typed over a selection wraps it (`==` makes a highlight).
- **Obsidian's keys:** ⌘L toggles a checkbox, ⌘K inserts a link, ⌘/ toggles a `%%` comment, ⌘D deletes the line. Option-click adds a cursor and ⌘⌥↑/↓ adds one above or below.
- **Suggestions:** `[[` lists your notes, `[[Note#` its headings, `[[#` this note's, `[[##` headings across the vault, `#` your tags, and `[^` the note's footnotes. They appear as you type.
- **Slash commands.** `/` at the start of a line or after a space lists the editor's commands: headings, lists, quote, callout, code block, table, date and time, and the formatting toggles.
- **Pasting.** Formatted text from a web page, a document or a spreadsheet comes in as Markdown: links, emphasis, headings, lists, tables. **Edit ▸ Paste as Plain Text** (⌘⇧V) pastes it plain. A pasted or dropped image is saved in the vault's attachment folder and linked, as Obsidian does.
- **A formatting bar** sits above a selection (copy, checklist, heading, quote, bold, italics, highlight colour, strikethrough, link, code, code block, clear formatting), with the Ask button below it, both in the note's own colours.
- **Folding.** An arrow in the margin folds a heading's section or a list item's children, and a note's folds come back the next time you edit it.
- **Callouts** look as they do on the page while you edit: the type's icon and title, and a folded one folded.

## Reading

- **Footnotes** render: `[^1]` is a numbered superscript and the notes are listed at the end, with a way back.
- **Highlight colours:** `==🔴text==` and the other colour emojis highlight in Obsidian's colours. Typing `==` in the editor offers them, and so does the formatting bar.
